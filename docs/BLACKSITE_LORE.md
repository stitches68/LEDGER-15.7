# BLACKSITE NETWORK / FIELD LORE

**“If it happened beyond the perimeter, the Broker has already heard about it.”**

LEDGER isn't Super Earth's acquisitions clerk. It's the R.E.A.P.E.R. Divers' unmarked market contact: anonymous requisitions, contract slips that don't quite exist, and credits that appear only after the job is logged. The look is charcoal, matte black and signal green; the language is terse, conspiratorial, and PMC-focused rather than military cosplay.

## Interfaces

- **BLACKSITE TERMINAL:** one public contact point. Members privately access their dossier, markets, missions, units, or the mentorship network without scrolling through dozens of commands.
- **BLACK MARKET CONTRACTS:** offers include base pay, short mission terms, evidence rules, and optional bonus objectives. Private draft allows officers to set repeatability, required missions and public goals.
- **BLACKSITE DISPATCH:** a pinned, edited message listing active public goals. Live accepted-report counts update after approved reports plus periodic refresh. Unlike a new post for every update, the dispatcher edits its existing message.
- **CONTRACTOR DOSSIER:** callsign, specialty, motto, biography, wallet, affiliated units and verified stats. User-written cosmetics do not change game results or balances.
- **CONTRACTOR MARKET / UNIT SUPPLY EXCHANGE:** personal vs. treasury purchasing, handled by authorized staff.
- **FIND A MENTOR:** quiet way for new divers to find someone to show them the ropes, with paid mentorship contracts only after trainee confirmation and officer approval.

## Easter eggs

`/intel` reveals occasional original in-world transmissions. The wording shifts by day. These messages are purely narrative: they never pay fictional credits, bypass review, spoof staff or reveal private information. The lore is original to this project and does not include artwork or copy borrowed from the user-provided reference bot.

## Visual boundaries

Discord's native selects and buttons cannot be fully reskinned; LEDGER uses green/success controls and coordinated green embed accents. The user's original Blacksite Credits image is bundled as-is in `assets/`, along with the emoji-sized copy. No S.C.O.U.T. branding or images are reused.
